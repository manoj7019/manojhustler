Common questions

Is this a real clinic?

No. Smile Care Dental Clinic is a fictional demo by ManojHustler, made to show the kind of site a small clinic can have.

Do I need an appointment?

On this demo, booking only opens WhatsApp. In the story of the clinic, booked visits are seen first and walk-ins wait for a free chair.

What would I bring to a real visit?

Any older dental notes and a list of medicines you take. The first minutes are a look and a conversation. Treatment starts only after you agree.

Will I get a reminder?

Not from this page. A real build stores the booking and sends WhatsApp reminders automatically.
